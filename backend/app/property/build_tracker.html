Loan officer commands

Loan officers read and request. They cannot edit estimates or flags, and cannot approve a case with an open flag.

  • Every command writes an audit event and returns structured output plus a short summary.
  • Low confidence, abstain, empty retrieval or provider failure always sets human review.
  • Wording is potential issue for review, never a legal conclusion.
